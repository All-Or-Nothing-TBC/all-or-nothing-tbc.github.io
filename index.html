Tier 6 Loot Court

The complete drop tables of Black Temple and Hyjal Summit — every boss drop, tier token, trash epic and crafting pattern — ranked on a 7-tier pricing ladder built from wowtbc.gg BIS lists for all 27 specs across the T6, Zul’Aman and Sunwell phases, cross-checked against WoWSims preset gear. Min DKP floors shown on every card — weekly income is 500 on a full clear.

The pricing ladder — highest tier the item ever reaches, top price first:
  1. P4 BIS — still best-in-slot when Sunwell is out; the SWP row shows which specs. Maximum hold value. Min 200.
  2. P3 BIS — best-in-slot this phase (including through the ZA catch-up patch), replaced by Sunwell gear. Min 100.
  3. ALT ONLY — never anyone’s #1; an upgrade option (chip #N = that spec’s slot ranking). Min 50.
  4. MEH — appears in no spec’s BIS list at any phase. Min 20 floor.
  5. GUILD GOLD — patterns are not DKP loot: they go to guild crafters or the AH for bank gold.

Minimums are floors for uncontested drops — open bidding above them sets the real price.

Earning DKP — everyone starts at 1,000 DKP on the day of the reset; a full clear then pays 500 per week:
  • +20 on time at raid start · +20 staying to raid end (each night)
  • +20 per farm boss — Hyjal 1–4 and Black Temple 1–6
  • +50 Mother Shahraz · +50 Illidari Council · +50 Illidan Stormrage
  • +70 Archimonde

Boss awards are repeated next to each boss header below.

Rows on each card: BIS = #1 pick now · ALT = ranked alternative now · SWP = specs still calling it BIS in the Sunwell phase. ✓ = WoWSims preset for that phase agrees; dashed chip = WoWSims-only pick. Chips are class-colored. Tier set pieces are shown as the tokens that actually drop.
